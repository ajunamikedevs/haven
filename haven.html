What Haven does

Everything you need for the week, and nothing extra.

One shared week

Practices, appointments and late shifts for the whole family, on one page, colored by person.

A dinner slot for every night

Pick from easy family meals or add your own. Busy nights get quick ideas first.

A grocery list that writes itself

Ingredients from the week’s dinners are added up and sorted by aisle, ready to share.

Made for the whole household

Add everyone who lives with you so each event shows whose it is at a glance.

How it works

One short loop, once a week.

  1. See the week

    Everyone’s plans for Monday to Sunday, in one scroll.

  2. Plan the dinners

    Tap an open night and choose a meal that fits how busy it is.

  3. Get the list

    Haven builds the grocery list. Check items off in the store or send it to whoever is shopping.

Most families finish this in about ten minutes on Sunday evening.

What Haven does

Everything for the week, and nothing extra.

One shared week

Practices, appointments and late shifts for the whole family on one page, colored by person.

A dinner slot every night

Pick from easy family meals or type your own. Busy nights get quick ideas first.

A list that writes itself

Ingredients are added up and sorted by aisle, ready to copy into a text.

Made for the household

Add everyone you live with so each plan shows whose it is at a glance.

How it works

One short loop, once a week.

Most families are done in about ten minutes on Sunday evening.

  1. See the week

    Everyone’s plans for Monday to Sunday, in one scroll.

  2. Plan the dinners

    Tap an open night and choose a meal that fits how busy it is.

  3. Get the list

    Haven builds the grocery list. Tick items off in the store or send it to whoever’s shopping.
